## How to Upload:
1. Save your flyer images with the exact filenames listed above
2. Place them in the `/public/images/` folder
3. Use `.jpg` or `.png` format
4. Make sure the filenames match exactly (case-sensitive)

## Current Placeholder Images:
The SVG placeholder images will be automatically replaced once you upload the actual images with the correct filenames.
