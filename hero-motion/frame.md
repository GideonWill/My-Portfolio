# Design direction

Deep navy is the still center of the system. Wine and electric blue appear as localized, luminous sources with generous negative space and softly layered depth. Use a restrained cinematic glow, delicate glass, and a few precise white details rather than neon clutter.

These compositions are decorative background plates only. Do not add visible text, interface labels, logos, audio, or page copy. Each loop should return to its opening visual state on the final sampled frame.

## Motion language

- Home breathes with a slow wine/blue aurora while small white particles drift.
- About lets three quiet points of light orbit inside fine concentric rings.
- Projects layers translucent glass planes in gentle parallax with restrained traveling streaks.
- Resume suggests a considered body of work with a few floating document-like forms.
- Contact keeps the calmest visual: two slow aurora lights and a soft shared pulse.
- Services draws three connecting lines between glowing nodes; it is used behind Home's existing capabilities section.

## Delivery constraints

Render each composition at 1920x1080 / 30 fps / 6 seconds. Encode site assets at 1280x720, muted, and under 2 MB each. Keep the source compositions and repeatable render script beside this design spec.
