# Ambient loop plan

All six frames are separate, text-free 1920x1080 compositions with a six-second, 30 fps cycle. Every motion returns to its starting state at the last sampled frame.

| Frame | Beat | Visual experience | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Aurora field | Wine and blue light drift through a quiet navy atmosphere; fine white particles give the existing portrait and copy a little depth. | Broad, reversible aurora drift and a gentle particle float. |
| About | Orbit | Three luminous points travel around delicate glass-like rings, suggesting curiosity and connected ideas. | Slow counter-orbiting rings with restrained light pulses. |
| Projects | Glass in motion | Layered translucent planes and thin light trails hint at a polished collection of digital work. | Cards float at different depths while light streaks draw across the frame. |
| Resume | Crafted layers | A few document-like planes and precise highlights suggest an organized, evolving practice without imitating a literal CV. | Soft document parallax and reversible glints. |
| Contact | Quiet invitation | Two broad aurora sources meet in a stable navy field, leaving calm negative space around the contact message. | Slow, low-contrast pulse and drift. |
| Services | Connected craft | Fine paths connect three luminous nodes, echoing the existing capabilities section's design-to-engineering story. | Paths draw and retract as nodes breathe. |
